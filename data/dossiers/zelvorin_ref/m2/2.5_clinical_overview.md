# 2.5 Clinical Overview

## 2.5.4 Overview of efficacy
In the pivotal study ZLV-301, zelvoretinib improved progression-free survival versus
standard of care (HR 0.62; 95% CI 0.49-0.78).

## 2.5.5 Overview of safety
The most common adverse reactions were diarrhoea, fatigue and ALT increase. Grade 3
hepatotoxicity occurred in 4.1% of patients.

## 2.5.6 Benefits and risks conclusions
The clinically meaningful PFS benefit outweighs the risks of hepatotoxicity and QT
prolongation, which are manageable with monitoring and dose modification. The
benefit-risk balance is considered positive.
