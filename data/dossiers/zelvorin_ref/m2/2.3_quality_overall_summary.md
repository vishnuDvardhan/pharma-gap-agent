# 2.3 Quality Overall Summary

## 2.3.S Drug substance
Zelvoretinib is a crystalline small molecule (Form A) manufactured by a five-step
synthesis. The drug substance specification controls identification, assay, related
substances, residual solvents and particle size.

## 2.3.P Drug product
Zelvorin 50 mg film-coated tablets are manufactured by dry granulation, compression and
film coating. The drug product specification controls assay, degradation products,
dissolution and uniformity of dosage units. Stability data support a shelf life of
36 months with no special storage conditions.
