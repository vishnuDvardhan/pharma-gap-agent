# 3.2.P.5.5 Characterisation of Impurities

The main degradation product ZLV-D1 (N-oxide) forms under oxidative stress. Its
structure was confirmed by LC-MS and NMR.

Degradation products are reported above the reporting threshold of 0.1% and
identified above the identification threshold of 0.2%, in line with ICH Q3B(R2) for a
maximum daily dose of 100 mg.
