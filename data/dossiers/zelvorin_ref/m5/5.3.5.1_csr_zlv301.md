# 5.3.5.1 Clinical Study Report ZLV-301 (synopsis)

Title: A randomised, open-label, phase III study of zelvoretinib versus standard of care
in adults with previously treated advanced solid tumours harbouring ZLV-1 alterations.

Sites: 142 sites in Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Poland, the United States and Canada.
No patients were enrolled in Asia.

Patients: 612 randomised 1:1.

Primary endpoint: progression-free survival by blinded independent central review.
Result: median PFS 9.4 vs 5.6 months; HR 0.62 (95% CI 0.49-0.78); p < 0.001.

Clinical investigators: 46 principal and sub-investigators across all sites.
