# 1.2 Regulatory Status, Site Registration and Waiver Request (India)

## Regulatory status
Zelvorin is approved in the European Union. A copy of the European Commission Decision
C(2026) 1402 granting the marketing authorisation dated 2 March 2026 is enclosed.

## Registration of overseas site
An application in Form 40 for registration of the manufacturing site Helvara Production
GmbH (Freiburg, Germany) and the product has been submitted, together with the
site master file and the GMP certificate issued by the competent authority.

## Request for waiver of local clinical trial
Under Rule 101 of the New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019, the applicant requests
a waiver of the local clinical trial in Indian subjects, because the drug is approved
in the European Union.
