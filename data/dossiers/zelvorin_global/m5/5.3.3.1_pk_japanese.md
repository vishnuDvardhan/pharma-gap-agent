# 5.3.3.1 Study ZLV-104: Pharmacokinetics in Japanese and Caucasian Healthy Subjects

Design: open-label, single and multiple dose (50 mg once daily) study in 24 Japanese
and 24 Caucasian healthy adult subjects, conducted in Tokyo and London.

Results: Geometric mean ratios (Japanese/Caucasian) were 1.08 for Cmax and 1.05 for
AUC at steady state. No dose adjustment is needed based on ethnicity.

Bridging rationale (ICH E5): zelvoretinib is not metabolised by polymorphic enzymes,
has linear PK and a wide therapeutic window, so it is considered ethnically
insensitive. Foreign efficacy data from ZLV-301 are proposed to be extrapolated to the
Japanese population.
