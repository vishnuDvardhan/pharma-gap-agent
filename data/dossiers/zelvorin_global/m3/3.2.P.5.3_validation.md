# 3.2.P.5.3 Validation of Analytical Procedures

HPLC-ZLV-01 (assay) and HPLC-ZLV-02 (degradation products) were validated according to
ICH Q2(R2).

| Characteristic | HPLC-ZLV-01 (assay) | HPLC-ZLV-02 (degradation products) |
|---|---|---|
| Specificity | No interference from placebo or degradants; peak purity passed | Resolution ≥ 2.0 between all specified degradants |
| Linearity / range | 70-130%, r = 0.9998 | LOQ-150% of limit, r = 0.9991 |
| Accuracy | Mean recovery 99.6% | Recovery 94-104% |
| Precision (repeatability) | RSD 0.4% | RSD 3.1% at limit |
| Intermediate precision | RSD 0.7% | RSD 4.2% |
| LOQ | - | 0.05% |
| Robustness | Passed | Passed |
