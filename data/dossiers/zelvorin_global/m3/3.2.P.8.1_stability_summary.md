# 3.2.P.8.1 Stability Summary and Conclusion

Three primary commercial-scale batches were placed on long-term and accelerated
stability in the proposed Alu/Alu blister. No significant change was observed at the
accelerated condition. All results remained within the shelf-life specification.

For Zone IVb markets, two batches were placed at 30°C/75% RH. A slight upward trend
in ZLV-D1 was seen; statistical evaluation per ICH Q1E supports a shorter shelf life.

Proposed shelf life (Zone II, 25°C/60% RH): 36 months
Proposed shelf life (Zone IVb, 30°C/75% RH): 24 months
Storage statement (Zone IVb): Do not store above 30°C. Store in the original package.
