# 3.2.P.3.5 Process Validation and/or Evaluation

Process validation was performed on three consecutive commercial-scale validation
batches (ZLV-P-001, ZLV-P-002, ZLV-P-003) at Helvara Production GmbH.

Critical steps evaluated: roller compaction (ribbon density), final blend uniformity,
compression (tablet hardness, weight variation) and film coating (weight gain).
All results met the predefined acceptance criteria. Continued process verification
will be applied after approval.
